BeHappy privacy policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

BeHappy is a free daily mood and planner app. You do not create an account. Your mood check-ins, three daily tasks, and optional notes are stored in a SQLite database on your device. BeHappy does not operate a backend that receives your journal contents.

Advertising

The free version may show Google AdMob banner ads on the Today and History screens (never over the check-in controls). AdMob may collect advertising identifiers, IP address, and approximate device information. In the EEA/UK, a consent form is shown before personalized ads are requested. See Google’s privacy policy.

Payments

Remove Ads is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment data. BeHappy only stores a local flag that ads were removed.

Notifications

Optional local reminders stay on your device. BeHappy does not send push notifications from a server in version 1.

Deletion

Uninstalling BeHappy deletes on-device data. There is no cloud copy to erase.
